Northfield Community Library: Member Handbook

Membership. Residents of Northfield can join for free with proof of address. Non-residents pay an annual fee of $40. Children under 16 need a parent or guardian to sign the application. Cards expire after three years and can be renewed in person or online.

Borrowing limits. Adults may have 15 items checked out at once. Teen cardholders may have 10 items. Children may have 8 items. Laptops count as three items toward the limit.

Loan periods. Books are lent for 21 days. DVDs are lent for 7 days. Magazines are lent for 14 days. Laptops are lent for 3 days and cannot be renewed. Audiobooks are lent for 21 days.

Renewals. Most items can be renewed twice, either online or by phone. Items with a waiting list cannot be renewed. Each renewal extends the loan by the original loan period.

Late fines. Books and magazines cost 25 cents per day, capped at $5 per item. DVDs cost $1 per day, capped at $10. Laptops cost $5 per day. Children's materials never collect fines. Borrowing privileges are suspended when unpaid fines reach $15.

Lost or damaged items. A lost item is charged at its replacement cost plus a $3 processing fee. Water-damaged items are treated as lost. If you find a lost item within 60 days and return it in good condition, the replacement cost is refunded, but the processing fee is not.

Opening hours. The main branch is open Monday to Thursday from 9 am to 8 pm, and Friday and Saturday from 9 am to 5 pm. On Sunday the main branch is open from 1 pm to 5 pm. All branches are closed on public holidays. The Eastside branch is open Tuesday to Saturday from 10 am to 6 pm.

Study rooms. Rooms can be booked up to 14 days in advance, for at most 2 hours per day per person. Each room holds up to 6 people. A reservation is released if nobody checks in within 15 minutes of the start time.

Printing. Black-and-white pages cost 10 cents and color pages cost 50 cents. The first 10 black-and-white pages each day are free for cardholders. You can print from a personal device through the web portal and collect the pages within 24 hours.

Computers and Wi-Fi. Public computers are available in 90-minute sessions, which can be extended once if nobody is waiting. The Wi-Fi network is called NorthfieldGuest, needs no password, and reaches into the parking lot.

Interlibrary loan. You can request books from other libraries for free, up to 5 requests per month. Items usually arrive within 7 to 10 days. Interlibrary items cannot be renewed.

Events. Toddler story time is every Wednesday at 10:30 am. The Teen Book Club meets on the first Thursday of every month at 4 pm. Free resume workshops run on the second Saturday of each month.

Donations. The library accepts donated books in good condition. It does not accept textbooks older than 5 years, encyclopedias, or magazines more than 1 year old. A donation drop box is located by the back entrance and is available during opening hours.

Accessibility. The building has an elevator, a large-print collection on the second floor, and hearing loops in the meeting room. Text-to-speech devices can be borrowed from staff. Service animals are welcome.

Code of conduct. Food is not allowed in reading areas, but covered drinks are fine. Phones must be silent in the quiet zones on the second floor.

Contact. Call 555-0142 or email help@northfieldlibrary.example.
